# Qualification

Score each channel on a 0–100 scale for:

- Fit: ICP suitability and economic viability.
- Readiness: assets, audience, traffic, funnel, team, and infrastructure.
- Gap: evidence that a suitable capability is underdeveloped.
- Trigger: a current reason to act.

Default total: `30% fit + 25% readiness + 25% gap + 20% trigger`. Keep weights configurable and retain component scores.

Disqualify or route to review when minimum fit, confidence, evidence count, or freshness is not met. Positive evidence of an active channel should contradict the gap rather than merely reduce confidence.

Phrase outreach as an observable opportunity: “We found X and Y, which suggests Z may be underused.” Never state “you are not doing Z” unless there is direct, current confirmation.

Evaluate precision per channel against human labels and outcomes. Tie evaluation to the historical evidence snapshot used at send time.
