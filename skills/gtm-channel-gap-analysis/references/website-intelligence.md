# Website intelligence

Discover pages from sitemaps, navigation, known resource paths, SERP results, and observed ad destinations.

Classify substantial assets into reports, original research, ebooks, guides, white papers, live/on-demand webinars, case studies, templates, and other resources. Do not equate blog volume with campaign-ready assets.

Classify gating as fully ungated, optional gate, summary ungated/full asset gated, fully gated, registration required, or unknown. Distinguish asset forms from newsletter, chat, and demo forms. Avoid submitting forms unless explicitly authorized.

For dates, prefer structured metadata and visible dates. Treat sitemap `lastmod` and search-engine dates as weaker evidence. Calculate recency and cadence per content class.

For segment pages, extract industry, persona, use case, geography, and company size. Measure whether segmentation is substantive by checking distinct pain points, proof, terminology, integrations, and CTAs.

Model conversions as paths: source page → CTA → destination → form/scheduler/signup. Capture CTA type, clicks, fields, tools, and friction.

Grade proof from logo-only evidence through named, quantified enterprise outcomes with attributed testimonials. Preserve the exact supporting excerpt.
